About

A Southern queer newsroom of one, for now

queerham started as a place to share queer stories from the South. Today it is a place to find out what is happening to our rights, what it means, and what to do about it.

Read more in Why queerham exists.

Editorial standards

  • Every post is labeled News, Explainer or Perspective. Opinion is always marked at the top.
  • News links to the source. The bill text, the court ruling, the agency rule, or the original reporting. Where possible, more than one outlet, from different perspectives.
  • We describe laws by what they do, using their official names first, and note what supporters and opponents say about them.
  • No endorsements. queerham does not endorse candidates or parties. The voting guide and polls are nonpartisan.
  • Independent sources only. Headlines in The Latest come from queer, nonprofit and community newsrooms. We never pull from national mainstream outlets.
  • If we are not sure, we say so. Unconfirmed items are marked as such or left out.
  • Every tracker shows a "last checked" date.

Corrections

If something here is wrong or out of date, tell me on Instagram at @queer.ham. Corrections are noted at the bottom of the post with the date, never quietly swapped in.

Share freely

Screenshot it, text it to your group chat, print the checklists. That is what it is for.
