Wins this year

Georgia / April 2026

Every anti-LGBTQ bill failed

All of the roughly 15 anti-LGBTQ bills filed in Georgia's 2026 session died, including limits on care, library penalties and a forced-outing bill.

Rough Draft Atlanta
Kentucky / April 2026

Zero anti-LGBTQ bills passed

Lawmakers filed about ten. None became law, a first in years, credited to steady organizing across the state.

Lexington Times
Florida / 2026 session

Four of five bills stopped

Advocates stopped or neutralized a Pride flag ban, a "Don't Say Gay or Trans at Work" bill and new lawsuits against teachers and doctors.

Equality Florida (advocacy group), session results
Federal / Nov. 2025

Marriage equality stands

The Supreme Court turned away the Kim Davis case asking it to overturn same-sex marriage. No justice publicly dissented.

U.S. Supreme Court docket
Virginia / July 2026

School facilities rule dropped

Gov. Spanberger ended a Youngkin-era rulemaking that would have limited trans students' access to school facilities and sports, saying the state had no legal authority to move forward.

Radio IQ (Virginia public radio)
Courts / 2026

Doctors, not officials, set care standards

A federal judge ruled HHS cannot invent its own standards of care to push providers out of Medicare and Medicaid, in the states that sued.
