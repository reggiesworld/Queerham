Your Voices

Share your story

What is it like to be queer where you live right now? The wins, the fear, the family dinners, the small-town Pride, the law that changed your plans. We want to hear it in your words.

  • You choose your name. Publish under your first name, initials, a pen name or anonymously. Your email is never published.
  • Nothing goes up automatically. We read every story, may edit for length and clarity, and email you before anything is published.
  • Your story, your experience. If you mention a law or a named person, we check the facts first.
  • Be safe. Leave out details that could identify you, your workplace or your family if that would put you at risk.
