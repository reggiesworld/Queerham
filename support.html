Questions

Will anything go behind a paywall?

No. Every page stays free for everyone. Membership pays for the site; it doesn't unlock it.

How do I cancel?

Anytime, in two clicks, from the link Stripe emails you with your receipt. You won't be charged again after you cancel.

Is my payment private?

Stripe handles the payment. queerham never sees or stores your card number. Your bank statement will show QH MEDIA, not the word "queer," so it stays discreet if you aren't out to everyone who sees your statements.

Is it tax-deductible?

No. queerham is not a registered nonprofit, so memberships are not tax-deductible.

Can I give once instead?

Not yet. For now it's monthly only. Message us on Instagram if you'd like to give a one-time gift.
