The law right nowDetails →
  • Gender-affirming care is a felony for anyone under 19 (SB 184).
  • Schools require restrooms by original birth certificate, and bar K-5 lessons on orientation or gender identity (HB 322).
  • Sports bar trans girls from girls' K-12 and college teams.
  • State law defines sex as sex at birth since Oct. 1, 2025 (SB 79).
  • No statewide nondiscrimination law. Birmingham's city ordinance covers sexual orientation and gender identity.
